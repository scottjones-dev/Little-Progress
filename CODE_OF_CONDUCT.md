# Code of Conduct

LittleProgress is used by families caring for small children, and built by people who volunteer their time. We want everyone to feel welcome and safe taking part.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Welcome people of every background, experience level, age, gender, ability, nationality and belief.
- Respect privacy. Never share anyone's personal information, and never share information about a child, real or not yours to share.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything that would put a child at risk.
- Spam, or pushing a commercial product into discussions.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commits) and anywhere a person is clearly speaking for the project.

## Reporting and what happens next

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, Scott Jones, at scottjones@alicesystems.co.uk. Reports are kept confidential and are looked at promptly and fairly.

The maintainer can warn, edit or remove comments and contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
